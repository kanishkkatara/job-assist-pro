How to Use
📋 Step 1 — Create Profiles
Create a profile for each job role you're applying to (e.g., "FDE", "TPM"). Fill in your personal details, work experience, skills, and upload your tailored resume PDF. The extension will extract text from your resume to use as context.
📑 Step 2 — Capture Job Descriptions
Navigate to a job listing on any platform (LinkedIn, Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, etc.). Click the JobAssist Pro extension icon, then click "Capture Job Description". The JD is saved as context for your next application.
✍️ Step 3 — Apply with AI Assist
On the application form page, click the extension icon and select your profile. Use "Auto-fill Form" to fill standard fields automatically. Use "Generate Cover Letter" to get an AI-crafted cover letter based on your profile and the JD. Use "Answer Questions" to get AI suggestions for open-ended application questions.
⚙️ Step 4 — Configure AI (Optional)
For AI-powered responses, go to Settings and add your OpenAI API key. Without an API key, the extension uses smart templates which still work well.
